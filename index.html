CAPABILITIES

Purpose-built for event analysis.

Less interface noise, more engineering context. Investigate recorded facts before reaching a conclusion.

01

Time signals & events

View analog and digital channels on one timeline with pan, zoom, trigger reference and precise event timing.

02

Dual measurement cursors

Use C1/C2, time differences, instantaneous readings and RMS to investigate transitions and signal behavior.

03

Electrical analysis

Inspect phasors, harmonics, symmetrical components, R–X impedance locus and the engineering value table.

04

Engineering transparency

Preserve original recording data, use supported RIO/XRIO protection overlays, and surface invalid or unsupported inputs.

WORKFLOW

From recording to insight.

A straightforward manual investigation path built around the way protection engineers work.

01

Open a COMTRADE record

Select a .cfg with its matching .dat. ArDiRec discovers available companion files and presents recording context.

02

Locate and measure the event

Inspect analog/digital traces, zoom around the trigger and place C1/C2 at relevant moments.

03

Cross-check electrical behavior

Compare RMS, phasors, impedance and table values with your equipment settings and engineering procedures.

OPEN ENGINEERING

Independent. Inspectable. Practical.

ArDiRec is built in C++20 and Qt Quick with indexed record access and performance-aware waveform rendering. The source is openly available under GPL-3.0-or-later.
