CAPABILITIES

Explore what happened, signal by signal.

Follow the recorded event and compare electrical measurements without losing your place on the timeline.

01

Waveforms & digital events

Inspect synchronized voltage, current and digital status signals with trigger-relative navigation.

02

Event timing

Place C1 and C2 around a transition to read signal values and measure elapsed time.

03

RMS, phasors & harmonics

Compare instantaneous and one-cycle RMS signals, phase relationships and harmonic content.

04

Impedance & values

Review R–X trajectories, supported protection-zone overlays and measurements in the value table.

WORKFLOW

From COMTRADE file to event review.

Open the recording, measure the sequence and inspect the electrical response.

01

Open your recording

Choose a .cfg file with its matching .dat. The recording and available companion metadata appear in the workspace.

02

Inspect the event timeline

Find a pickup or trip, zoom into the interval and position C1/C2 to measure the timing.

03

Review electrical measurements

Switch to RMS, phasor, harmonics, impedance and table views to examine the recorded behavior.
