# Community Conduct

ardirec is an engineering project. Participation should be professional, evidence-driven and respectful.

- Critique code, calculations and assumptions rather than people.
- Provide reproducible evidence for technical disagreements when possible.
- Do not harass, threaten, demean or discriminate against contributors.
- Do not publish private operational data or files without permission.
- Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.
